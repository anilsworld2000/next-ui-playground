# Finance Calculators Decisions

## Route naming

Use the lowercase kebab-case `/finance-calculators` route with kebab-case child routes, while displaying the title-case name “Finance Calculators” in navigation and the dashboard registry.

## Shared navigation

Use the shared vertical sidebar pattern for switching between calculator routes. Keep the landing cards as direct links so each calculator is reachable both from the landing page and from any child route.

## Currency formatting

Keep `formatCurrency` local to this micro app because INR display policy belongs to its UI boundary. Calculation functions continue to return plain numbers without presentation formatting.

## Future Value behavior

Future Value supports Lump Sum, Monthly SIP, and Step-up SIP with live results and inline input errors. Rates are entered as percentages and converted to decimals at the calculation boundary. Step-up SIP rejects a zero expected annual return because the shared formula does not define that case. Fractional durations are accepted; the simple invested-total summary prorates deposits during the final partial year.

## SIP Planner behavior

SIP Planner reverses the shared fixed and step-up SIP formulas to calculate the required month-one amount from a target. It keeps fixed/step-up mode and form state local, recalculates through a deferred value, and shows the year-one and final-year monthly contribution trajectory. Percent inputs are converted to decimals at the calculation boundary. Zero annual return remains valid for fixed SIP but is rejected in step-up mode because the shared step-up formula does not define it; equal annual growth and step-up factors are delegated to the library's limit branch.

## Retirement Corpus behavior

Retirement Corpus defaults to the supplied example assumptions: monthly expense 100000, multiplier 16.8, 22 years to retirement, 26 drawdown years, 12% nominal pre-tax return, and 8% inflation. Tax haircut defaults to 6% and remains a one-time divisor in the fixed-duration formula. The primary fixed-duration target is distinct from perpetual safe-withdrawal and real-return comparisons.

Current savings are projected to retirement for SIP shortfall calculations, while the progress percentage compares current savings directly with the fixed-duration target. Zero years to retirement is allowed; a positive shortfall cannot be funded by future SIPs. Undefined perpetual denominators are shown as unavailable independently. Equal nominal return and inflation with positive drawdown makes only the fixed-duration target unavailable with an inline warning. Shared formula behavior is unchanged.

## SWP Simulator behavior

SWP Simulator calls the shared monthly `simulateSWP` function and derives route-local yearly snapshots. Positive durations must resolve to whole months; complete years and a final partial year each have one table row. If depletion occurs, the depletion period is converted to elapsed years and months, and its yearly row is highlighted. The depletion-period withdrawal is included in that year's total as recorded by the shared simulator. Required numeric fields start blank, withdrawal growth defaults to 0%, expected annual return is constrained to 0%-100%, and withdrawal growth is constrained to -100%-100%. Simulations are capped at 1,000 years to bound client-side work. Non-finite results are surfaced as an explicit inline error rather than displayed as currency.

## Finance chart behavior

Use Recharts for route-local responsive charts because the project had no existing chart library. Use semantic theme CSS variables for all chart colors and keep INR formatting at the display boundary. The SWP chart keeps a zero point for depletion rather than dropping the series. Retirement comparison charts omit unavailable methods and show their explanatory reasons instead of suggesting a value of zero.

## Monte Carlo retirement behavior

The shared calculation library models each year's return with a Normal distribution generated by the Box-Muller transform and clamps only the lower tail at -95%; it deliberately does not model lognormal returns. Accumulation uses `annualReturn / 12`, adds each month's SIP after growth, and increases the SIP once per year. The tax haircut is applied once at retirement. Drawdown also uses `annualReturn / 12`, grows before subtracting each month's withdrawal, and floors a depleted balance at zero. Fan-chart and outcome percentiles use linear interpolation over sorted values. `runMonteCarlo` defaults to 2,000 paths and requires a positive integer iteration count. Invalid, non-finite, or out-of-domain simulation inputs throw `RangeError`: amounts must be nonnegative, durations whole nonnegative years, step-up at least -100%, standard deviation nonnegative, inflation greater than -100%, and tax haircut in [0%, 100%). No arbitrary iteration cap is imposed.

## Retirement Stress Test behavior

The route reuses the Retirement Corpus assumptions and derives its contribution shortfall from the same fixed-duration target, current-corpus projection, and `requiredStepUpSIP` formula. The first retirement-year withdrawal is today's monthly expense multiplied by the expense multiplier and inflated to retirement. Simulations run only on button activation inside a dedicated Web Worker; changing any assumption terminates the active worker and clears stale results. The selector offers 500, 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, and 10,000 paths, with 2,000 as the default. Durations must be whole years and their sum is limited to 1,000 years; the product of path count and horizon is limited to 2,000,000 year-paths to bound worker memory. The UI colors success rates green at 85% or higher, amber from 60% to below 85%, and red below 60%; these thresholds are explanatory visual categories, not recommendations.
